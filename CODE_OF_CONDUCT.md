# Code of Conduct

FailLens is an open project where contributors must communicate respectfully and focus reviews on the work, not the person.

## Expected behavior

- Be welcoming to people with different levels of experience.
- Give specific, constructive, and patient feedback.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's sensitive log data.
- Accept reasonable review decisions and discuss disagreements with evidence.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information are not accepted.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainer through the contact method listed in the GitHub security policy. Reports will be reviewed as confidentially and promptly as possible. The maintainer may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed.
